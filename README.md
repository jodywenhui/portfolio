### About the project
- I recently worked on a project analyzing customer churn in a telecom subscription dataset to understand why customers leave and how businesses can intervene earlier.
- The objective was to identify patterns in customer behavior and build a predictive model that estimates churn risk before customers cancel their service.
- Through exploratory analysis, I examined factors such as contract type, tenure, billing patterns, and service subscriptions to uncover key drivers of churn. I then developed a classification model to estimate churn probability and identify high-risk customer segments.
- The results showed that customers with shorter tenure and flexible contract types were significantly more likely to churn. I also built a dashboard to visualize churn trends and help stakeholders monitor retention risk across different customer groups.
- This project highlights how data-driven insights can support proactive retention strategies and improve customer lifecycle management.
